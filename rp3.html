Only 8.3% of masters win consistently

We analysed every official result from Bled 2026: 4,303 rowers and 6,245 timed boats. Two thirds of the field never won a heat. A small group won again and again.

The gap to the top is not more metres. It's better strokes, held for longer.

At this level everybody trains hard. What separates the top group is how well they keep their stroke together when it hurts: consistency, and knowing exactly what breaks down under fatigue.

That is precisely what RP3 measures and Beat Your Best analyses.

Top masters rowers in categories D, E and F already train on RP3 with Beat Your Best, and they win. It works.
